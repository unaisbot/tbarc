Who we are

Tropical Biodiversity Action & Research Center

TBARC is dedicated to conserving biodiversity through research, education, and community action. It serves as a platform for scientific studies, awareness programs, and sustainable solutions to address ecological challenges.

Our direction

Guided by purpose.
Driven by action.

Our vision and mission guide our work towards ecological resilience, informed communities and long-term benefits for people and the planet.

01 / Vision

A future where nature thrives

To conserve biodiversity through innovative, science-based and community-driven approaches, fostering harmony between humans and nature while ensuring ecological resilience, sustainable livelihoods, and intergenerational environmental stewardship.

02 / Mission

Turning knowledge into conservation

To protect species and habitats through research, education, and collaboration, engage communities in conservation, build awareness, promote sustainable practices, and develop leadership, ensuring ecological balance and long-term benefits for people and the planet.
