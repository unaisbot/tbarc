Who we are

About Tropical Biodiversity Action and Research Centre

Tropical Biodiversity Action and Research Centre (TBARC) is dedicated to conserving biodiversity through research, education, and community action. We believe that ecological well-being and human prosperity are interconnected, and our mission is to safeguard this delicate balance by addressing pressing conservation challenges.

From restoring degraded habitats and protecting endangered species to fostering environmental awareness and community participation, we aim to build a sustainable future for both people and nature. Our work is rooted in scientific research, innovative conservation tools, and inclusive community-driven approaches that encourage participation at every level.

Our direction

A clear purpose for people and nature.

Our vision and mission guide the way we connect ecological knowledge with practical conservation action.

01 / Vision

A future where nature thrives

To conserve biodiversity through innovative, science-based and community-driven approaches, fostering harmony between humans and nature while ensuring ecological resilience, sustainable livelihoods, and intergenerational environmental stewardship.

02 / Mission

Turning knowledge into conservation

To protect species and habitats through research, education, and collaboration; engage communities in conservation; build awareness; promote sustainable practices; and develop leadership for ecological balance and long-term benefits for people and the planet.

What we work for

Connecting science with meaningful action.

TBARC brings research, conservation practice and community participation together to respond to the interconnected challenges facing biodiversity and ecosystems.

01

Evidence-led research

Support field-based ecological studies, biodiversity documentation, monitoring and scientific analysis. We encourage the use of appropriate tools and methods to understand species, habitats, ecosystem processes and emerging environmental pressures.

02

Species and habitat conservation

Promote practical conservation approaches that protect native biodiversity, restore degraded habitats, address invasive species and support the long-term ecological health of landscapes and wetlands.

03

Education and awareness

Make ecological knowledge accessible through workshops, learning programmes, public communication and nature-based activities that help people understand biodiversity and the importance of conservation.

04

Community participation

Encourage people, students, volunteers and local groups to take part in biodiversity documentation, citizen science, awareness initiatives and collaborative conservation activities.

05

Capacity building and collaboration

Create opportunities for training, internships, research engagement and knowledge exchange while building partnerships with educational institutions, researchers, government agencies and civil society organisations.

06

Innovation for conservation

Explore the responsible use of technologies such as GIS, remote sensing, digital platforms and mobile applications to strengthen ecological monitoring, data sharing and informed decision-making.
